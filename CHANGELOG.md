# Changelog

## 0.3.0 beta — 2026-10-14

- Automatically preserve ordinary or experimental native minecart physics for each world.
- Ordinary minecarts keep the standard speed and network updates; experimental worlds preserve their own speed rule.
- Fix rail projection and history handling for ordinary carts and slopes.
- Preserve chain linking, visible models, five-cart limit, powered-rail driving, driver-exit braking and persisted couplings.
- Validate 17 automated tests plus 1,935 experimental and 1,759 ordinary real-Paper simulation checks, including restarts.

## 0.2.0 beta

- Add visible iron-chain couplings using vanilla BlockDisplay models without client mods or a resource pack.

## 0.1.0 beta

- Initial chain linking, powered-rail driving, safety stops, detaching and persistence for Paper 26.3 experimental minecarts.
